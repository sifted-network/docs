# Product Application Scenarios

## Core Application Areas

This product is mainly applicable to **digital marketing and sales scenarios**. After loading the standard SOP for sales personnel, it can achieve the following core functions:

### 1. Channel Partner Identification and Outreach

- **Function Description**: Identify and match channel partners on social media that align with the company's positioning
- **Application Scenarios**:
  - Automatically screen potential partners
  - Establish initial contact via private messages
  - Obtain corporate email information through legal channels
  - Build business partnerships in batches

### 2. KOL Identification, Matching, and Engagement

- **Function Description**: Precisely identify and match influencers suitable for brand positioning
- **Application Scenarios**:
  - Multi-dimensional evaluation (followers, engagement rate, content quality)
  - Precise matching for vertical niche KOLs
  - Auto-generate collaboration proposal templates
  - Build long-term KOL resource database

### 3. High-Net-Worth Customer Identification, Screening, and Intelligent Engagement

- **Function Description**: Identify high-value customers and conduct intelligent engagement
- **Application Scenarios**:
  - Multi-dimensional data screening for high-net-worth individuals
  - Personalized demand analysis and matching
  - Intelligent recommendation of engagement strategies
  - Customer tier management and follow-up

## Workflow

1. **Configuration Phase**: Load sales personnel standard SOP and product positioning parameters
2. **Identification Phase**: System automatically scans social media platforms
3. **Matching Phase**: Intelligent matching based on preset rules
4. **Outreach Phase**: Auto-generate private message/email templates and execute outreach
5. **Follow-up Phase**: Record interaction data and optimize matching model

## Applicable Industries

- Brand marketing companies
- E-commerce platforms
- Financial service institutions
- Premium consumer goods industry
- SaaS software service providers
